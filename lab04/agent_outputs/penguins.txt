Penguins

Penguins are flightless seabirds that live mostly in the Southern Hemisphere. They are excellent swimmers, using their flipper-like wings to move through the water.

Penguins eat fish, krill, and squid. Their black-and-white coloring helps camouflage them: dark backs blend into the ocean when viewed from above, while white bellies blend with the bright surface when viewed from below.

There are many penguin species, including emperor, king, and little penguins. They live in a range of habitats, from Antarctic ice to temperate coastlines. Some species are threatened by climate change, habitat loss, pollution, and reduced food supplies.
